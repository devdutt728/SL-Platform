# Mail Draft For Role 5 / Role 6 Communication

**Suggested subject:** Role 5 / Role 6 Recruitment Workflow, GL Portal Actions, and Mail Visibility

Team,

Please note the current working scope and process for Role 5 / Role 6 users in the Recruitment System.

Role 5 and Role 6 users are currently handled in the same workflow and function as `GL / L2 owners` for assigned candidates.

Your primary working area is the `GL Portal`, where your role is focused on:

- interview assessment and feedback submission
- sprint review and decision-making
- opening requests for existing openings
- monitoring relevant internal workflow mails

Current feature scope:

- In `Assessments`, you can review your assigned interview queue, mark interview status as `taken` or `not_taken`, save draft feedback, submit final feedback, and download the assessment PDF after submission.
- In `Sprint Reviews`, you can review submitted sprint work, check the sprint brief and attachments, record score/comments, and submit the final decision as `advance` or `reject`.
- In `Opening Requests`, you can raise requests only against existing openings. You cannot approve or reject those requests.

Important access clarification:

- Candidate 360 is currently available to you in `view-only` mode.
- Lifecycle actions in Candidate 360 remain restricted to HR / privileged roles in the current page access model.

Sprint assignment clarification:

- Sprint assignment is now supported by backend rules for the assigned `L2 owner`, subject to ownership validation and the one-active-sprint rule.
- Reviewer reassignment remains an HR-only action.
- If sprint assignment is not visible in your current UI, please coordinate with HR / Superadmin instead of assuming access is missing in error.

Key internal mails relevant for Role 5 / Role 6 users:

- `Candidate assigned to you for L2 review`
- stage handoff mails for `L2 shortlist` and `Sprint`
- internal copy when `L2 slot options` are sent to the candidate
- internal mail when `Interview feedback` is submitted
- internal mail when `Sprint submission` is received
- internal mail when `Sprint review` is completed
- stale-stage internal follow-up for L2-related stages

Candidate-facing mails in the overall workflow include:

- application link / application received mails
- CAF / basic details link and reminder mails
- assessment link mail
- interview slot and schedule mails
- sprint assignment, reminder, and overdue mails
- offer and joining workflow mails

Important note on opening requests:

- Opening requests currently create workflow records and statuses in the system.
- Based on the current implementation, dedicated opening-request mail notifications are not yet part of this route flow.

Please use the GL Portal as the primary operating screen and ensure feedback, sprint reviews, and request details are completed accurately and on time.

For any issue related to permissions, sprint ownership, reviewer reassignment, opening request approval, or workflow correction, please connect with HR / Superadmin.
